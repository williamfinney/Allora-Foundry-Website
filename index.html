How We Work

We're not a fund. We're a build partner.

Allora Foundry doesn't write investment checks — yet. Instead, we invest our own time and expertise, structured around three ways of working with you.

For early-stage founders

Build for Equity

If you have a real idea and the drive to run with it, we'll build your app or product in exchange for a small equity stake — backed by hands-on marketing and sales guidance. We only win if you do.

For anyone who needs an app

App Build + Managed Care

We build your app for an upfront fee — scoped to your project and worked out together — then keep it running for a negotiated monthly fee covering updates, hosting, monitoring, and day-to-day organization so you never have to think about it.

For large, ambitious builds

Outsourced Engineering

When a project needs serious backend work beyond our core team — and it's a product we genuinely believe in — we bring in outside senior engineers and manage the work for a fee.
